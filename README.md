# Europe Trip Planner

A single-page travel planner for 56 cities in 36 European countries — the companion to the China & Hong Kong planner. Open `index.html` (or the one-file `europe-trip-planner.html`) in any browser — no server or install needed.

## What you enter
1. **Countries** you'll visit, grouped by region: Western (UK, Ireland, France, Belgium, Netherlands), Central (Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, Hungary, Slovenia), Nordic & Baltic (Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Iceland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania), Southern (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Malta), Balkans & South-East (Croatia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, Albania, North Macedonia, Romania, Bulgaria, Türkiye) and Eastern (Russia — shown with the Australian Government's "Do not travel" advice). Ukraine and Belarus are not included: both are under "Do not travel" advice.
2. **Cities** in those countries (one-tap suggestions per country), and the places you want to see in each — or leave it to the planner's top-rated picks
3. **Nights in each city**, with our suggested number beside each (one tap applies them all); the trip length follows automatically
4. **Dates & flights**: optional start date, arrival and departure cities, landing/take-off times and flight numbers, and how you'll travel between cities (train, flight, coach, hire car or ferry, with optional times) — or tick "Recommend the best city order and transport"
5. **Trip style**: sightseeing, culture & history, food & cafés, Instagram & photography, nature, adventure, Islamic heritage, relaxing, shopping, family — or your own
   - plus an optional free-text box — e.g. *“historical buildings and views, but no museums or long day trips”* or *“mum has bad knees, avoid stairs”*. The planner shows what it understood, favours matching places, leaves out what you don't want (except places you picked yourself) and lists what it left out

Plus pace, hotel budget, passport (Australian / other) and home airport (Sydney / elsewhere).

## What you get
- **Hour-by-hour plan you can edit** for every day: breakfast (with halal tips for hotel buffets), sights, halal lunch, a named café stop, a golden-hour **photo stop**, dinner, the evening activity and travel — plus flights, train times and Friday Jumu'ah. Add sights, halal restaurants, cafés, photo spots or anything of your own
- **Recommendations in every city**: top-rated sights (★), halal restaurants, mosques, cafés & desserts, Instagram-worthy spots with the best time to shoot, hotels by budget, the best area to stay, and local dishes (with what to avoid)
- **Transport that reflects Europe's real network**: Eurostar only where it runs, rail with researched times and fares for ~150 routes (including the Bergen Line and Sapsan), flights to islands, the Helsinki–Tallinn ferry, the Split–Dubrovnik catamaran in summer (bus out of season), Baltic and Balkan coaches where trains are slow or missing, and no rail or road into Russia from the EU
- **Entry rules by zone**: the Schengen 90-in-180 day count, the EU Entry/Exit System, ETIAS status, the UK ETA, Ireland, Türkiye, the Western Balkans and Russia — tailored for Australian passports (as checked November 2026)
- **Season and holiday warnings** from your dates: summer heat and crowds, Ferragosto, Christmas closures and markets, Alpine and Nordic winter, white nights (with high-latitude prayer-time advice), Santorini off-season, Ramadan and Eid
- **Maps**: a built-in route map that zooms to your trip (works offline and in print), a street map of every stop by day, and Google Maps links
- **Before you go**: visas, flights from Sydney, Australian reciprocal health care, face-covering laws, insurance, apps, money, tips and a saved checklist
- **Phrases & halal cards** in the 28 languages on your route
- **Weather & what to wear**: a live forecast for each day once you're within about two weeks of travel (Open-Meteo), and typical highs, lows and rainy days for your month before that; each day gets a weather line with what to wear — layers for mountain tops, grippy shoes for wet cobbles, covered shoulders and knees for churches and mosques, swimwear for beach days. A weather column in At a glance and a month-by-month table for every city on your route
- **Download PDF**: an A4 PDF that looks like the planner page — every tab (plan, map, weather, hotels, before you go, phrases), each section and each city starting a new page, never cut through a line of text, light theme even if your phone is in dark mode — made in the browser — on phones it opens a dialog to save it (iPhone: Save to Files or Books; Android: Files, Drive or Downloads). Needs internet the first time to load the PDF tools
- **Save to phone**: a script-free HTML copy of the whole plan that opens offline in any phone's file viewer, saved through the iPhone/Android share sheet or as a download
- **Print** (auto-fitted to A4) and **Copy as text**
- **Hotels — three picks per city**: ★ Best pick, Recommended pick and Low budget pick, all chosen for consistently good reviews, with typical nightly prices and distance from the centre
- **Your hotel**: pick one of the three on each city card (or in the Hotels tab with “Choose this hotel”), or choose “Another hotel…” and type its name or address (pick it from the OpenStreetMap matches), or paste a Google/Apple Maps link or coordinates for an exact pin. Short share links (maps.app.goo.gl) can't be read by a web page, so it explains how to copy the coordinates instead; if a hotel isn't listed you can place it near a landmark (approximate). The card shows where you're planning from with a “check the pin” link, and the plan updates straight away. Press **Build / update my itinerary** and every direction, walking time, nearest halal meal, day map and the PDF are worked out from that hotel
- **≈A$ next to every price** (euros, pounds, francs, kroner, złoty, forint…), using an editable rate (default €1 = A$1.64, mid-2026); other currencies convert via the euro
- **Top picks** on every city card adds the highest-rated places you haven't chosen yet, plus 1–2 Instagram-worthy photo spots timed for their best light; every photo spot can also be picked from the city's “📸 Instagram spots” list
- **Add my own idea**: type it the way you'd say it — *“day trip to Florence”* (from Milan), *“Lake Como”*, *“sunset at the Duomo rooftop”*, *“Shakespeare's Globe”* — and press Add. Optional “Time there” and “When” selectors override what it reads from your words. The planner recognises its own cities (day trips get the best transport, travel time each way, highlights and a halal lunch there, with a warning if the day is too long), its own places and photo spots, timing words (morning, sunset, 2 hours, full day), pasted Google Maps links, and otherwise looks it up on Wikipedia/OpenStreetMap — offering a pick list when there are several matches, and “add without a pin” or “put it near…” when nothing is found — then plans it with directions and halal food
- **Day trips on each city card**: “Day trips from …” lists nearby cities reachable in about 3½ h or less (with time each way and the best transport) plus the city's out-of-town sights (Versailles, Pompeii, Sintra…). Choose how long (full day, half day, or 2–6 hours there), when (best time, morning, afternoon, evening incl. after dark), which day (or the best day) and how to travel. Trips are timed door to door and built in. Day trips use the train, coach or ferry; a hire car is suggested only where there's no public transport, or if you choose it
- **Anything else? (step 6)**: type requests before building, one per line, in the same words as “Change my plan”. A live preview shows what each will add, move out or why it can't be done, and the summary lists the outcome of every day trip and request with a link to its day
- **Change my plan**: a box above the tabs where you type a change in your own words — e.g. *“Add a trip from Florence to Venice on 15 May from 8am till 7pm”*, *“Add the Pantheon on day 9 afternoon”*, *“Dinner at 7pm on day 3”*, *“Keep the evening of 12 May free”*, *“Remove lunch on day 9”*. It reads the day (date, day number or weekday), times (“5pm till 10pm”, “afternoon”, “4 hr”), the place or city and any transport you name, then shows the worked-out change before you add it: transport there and back door to door with fares and the alternative, time at the destination, sights, the view after dark, halal food (an early halal meal before you leave when the destination has none) and what it replaces. If the window is too short it offers to start earlier. Added changes update the day, At a glance, print and PDF at once, are saved in the browser and can be undone. Curated day-trip trains (station, last train back) can be added to `OUTINGS` in `routes.js`; otherwise the intercity estimates are used. If the page is published on claude.ai, requests it can't read can be passed to Claude (the viewer is asked first)

## Files
- `index.html` — page and styles
- `data.js` — trip types, countries and border zones; `cities-*.js` — the city database (edit these to add destinations)
- `climate.js` — typical monthly highs, lows, rainy days and what-to-wear notes for every city
- `hotels.js` — three hotel picks per city with locations and prices, plus exchange rates for the A$ conversions
- `routes.js` — intercity routes, ferries and flights from Sydney
- `map-data.js` — simplified land and border outlines (Natural Earth, public domain), generated by `tools/build-map-data.js`
- `app.js` — form, itinerary engine and rendering
- `europe-trip-planner.html` — everything in one file, generated by `python3 tools/build.py`
- `tools/REFRESH.md`, `tools/refresh-log.md`, `tools/smoke-test.js` — the daily data refresh: checklist, log and check

## Daily data refresh
A scheduled Claude session re-checks the data every morning (Sydney time): exchange rates and entry rules every day, and places, hotels, halal food, cafés, photo spots and routes for about 2 cities a day in rotation (every city roughly once a month). It follows `tools/REFRESH.md`, records what it changed in `tools/refresh-log.md`, and updates the **Data last refreshed** date shown at the bottom of the page, in the PDF/printout and in the text export (`DATA_INFO.refreshed` in `data.js`).

Prices, opening hours and entry rules change often: confirm on official sites before booking.

## Using it on iPhone and Android
File previews (Files, Mail, WhatsApp) don't run JavaScript, so open the planner in Safari or Chrome from a web address — e.g. host this repo with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from a branch) and open https://faraz-yarkhan.github.io/EuropeItinirary/. Then use **Download PDF** or **Save to phone** to keep your itinerary for offline use. In-app browsers (WhatsApp, Instagram, Gmail) often block downloads: open the page in Safari or Chrome first.
